    This page runs exact copies of the "SMS Macro checker" sheet's formulas: column B (Safe?), column C (Fixed version, including the sheet's TRIM, which squeezes repeated spaces; one addition: accented letters like í or á become i or a instead of being deleted) and the columns D to H checks. GSM-7 messages fit 160 characters in one segment (153 per segment when split). One character outside GSM-7, such as a curly apostrophe, em dash, bullet or emoji, switches the whole message to Unicode: 70 characters in one segment, 67 when split.
    
    
    Cost uses $0.004 per segment, matching the sheet. The characters { } [ ] ~ ^ | \ € are valid GSM-7 but count as 2 each.
