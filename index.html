      Why this matters: texts are paid for in pieces called segments. A normal text fits 160 characters in each piece. One special character, like a curly apostrophe (’), a long dash (—), a bullet (•) or an emoji, switches the whole text to a format that fits only 70 characters per piece. The same message can then cost 2 to 3 times more. This tool swaps those characters for plain ones.
